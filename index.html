My Passport

Prototype for youth in foster care

Every time a kid in foster care moves, new adults need to know things about them, and kids end up retelling their story again and again. My Passport lets a young person tell it once, in their own words, and decide what gets shared.

It’s private by design. Everything stays on the device, with no account, no server, and nothing sent to an AI, because this information belongs to minors. Every section has a share switch, and there’s a place to name topics they’d rather not discuss yet, with no details required.
