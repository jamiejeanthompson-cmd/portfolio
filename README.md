## Portfolio Hub (demo)

**[Open the live demo](https://jamiejeanthompson-cmd.github.io/portfolio/portfolio-hub-demo.html)**

A sanitized recreation of a portfolio management application I designed and built with Claude Code for a real delivery organization running 70+ concurrent initiatives. Every company, person, and number in this version is fictional.

- **Board:** every initiative as a flight strip moving through a seven-phase lifecycle, with health, value, next milestone, and any decision waiting on leadership
- **Intake:** new requests scored on value, urgency, strategic fit, and effort before anyone commits capacity
- **Capacity:** a team-by-month view that flags over-commitment before it becomes a missed date
- **Executive report:** generated from the live data, so leaders get the picture without anyone chasing status updates

The original became the delivery organization's central hub for initiative tracking, capacity planning, and automated executive reporting, and it replaced third-party software we would otherwise have paid for.

## My Passport

**[Open the app](https://jamiejeanthompson-cmd.github.io/portfolio/my-passport.html)** or **[see the example](https://jamiejeanthompson-cmd.github.io/portfolio/my-passport.html#example)**

A prototype for young people in foster care. Every time a kid moves, new adults need to know things about them, and kids end up retelling their story again and again. My Passport lets a young person tell it once, in their own words, and decide what gets shared.

Design choices that matter:

- **Privacy first.** Everything stays on the device. No account, no server, and nothing is sent to an AI. This data belongs to minors, so it shouldn't leave their hands.
- **The young person is in control.** Every section has a share switch. "My people" starts private by default, so other people's names aren't shared unless the youth chooses to share them.
- **No details needed.** There's a place to name topics they'd rather not discuss yet, without explaining why.
- **Help is built in.** The opening screen points to trusted adults, the Childhelp hotline, and 911.

Next step: test it with former foster youth and a CASA program, and build what they say is missing.
