My Passport

Your story, in your words.

Every time you move, new people need to know things about you. This lets you tell them once, your way, and only what you choose.

  • 🔒It stays on this device.Nothing you type is sent anywhere. There’s no account and no sign-in.
  • 🎚️You decide what gets shared.Every section has a switch. If it’s off, it stays out of the version you show people.
  • ⏭️Skip anything.There are no wrong answers and nothing is required. Come back and change it whenever you want.
  • 🚫Leave out private details.Don’t put passwords, Social Security numbers, case numbers, or addresses here.

If something isn’t okay where you’re living, tell your caseworker, your CASA or guardian ad litem, a teacher, or another adult you trust.

You can also call or text the Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline at 1-800-422-4453, any time. If you’re in danger right now, call 911.
